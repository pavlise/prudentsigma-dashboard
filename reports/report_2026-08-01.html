HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT: Friday's July Employment Situation report (Aug 7) is the pivotal print of the week. June payrolls came in at just +57,000 versus a +113,000 consensus, roughly half of expectations, with unemployment holding at 4.2%. A second consecutive soft print (consensus likely to sit in the 60–90K range) would cement a labor-market deceleration narrative and pull forward Fed rate-cut pricing meaningfully — historically, back-to-back payroll misses between FOMC meetings have preceded 50bp cuts (the July–Sept 2024 precedent). The asymmetry favors a dovish surprise: a weak print likely triggers a rates rally (10Y yields easing off their 4.74% high) and a broad-based equity/gold bid, while a strong beat risks reinforcing the "yields stay higher for longer" theme currently pressuring Tech and Utilities, and could reverse this week's DXY weakness. Given the current 30Y at post-2007 highs, bond-market reaction is likely to be the primary transmission channel into equities and gold on the day.
