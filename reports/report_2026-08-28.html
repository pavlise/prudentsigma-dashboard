HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT — Warsh's Jackson Hole Speech (today):
This is Warsh's first major address as Fed Chair, delivered just three weeks before a live FOMC meeting where markets assign roughly 1-in-3 odds to a hawkish outcome against a backdrop of 3.4% inflation and a visibly softening labor market. He has pre-committed to a "high-altitude," structural framing rather than near-term guidance, but any deviation — even a rhetorical tilt toward inflation vigilance or toward labor-market concern — will be read as the tiebreaker for September policy. Positioning is unusually clean heading in (P/C ratios neutral, VIX near 52-week lows), which means the asymmetric risk sits with a surprise: a hawkish inflation-focused tone risks a fast unwind in the crowded gold/BTC/silver overbought complex and a yield spike that pressures NDX's rate-sensitive growth names; a dovish acknowledgment of labor softening would extend the current risk-on regime and could push gold through its 52-week high (5,318.40) while further compressing the DXY. Given softening JOLTS/ISM data due the following week and CPI on September 11, the path of least resistance into the FOMC still favors a cut, but Warsh's own words today are the single biggest swing factor for the next five sessions.
