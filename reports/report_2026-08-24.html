This is a defensive-to-cyclical barbell rather than a clean risk-on or risk-off rotation — money is favoring earnings-resilient healthcare and inflation-hedge energy over rate-sensitive utilities/REITs, consistent with a market bracing for yield-curve pressure rather than a growth scare.
