HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT: September 5 Nonfarm Payrolls. This print has become the single most important data point standing between markets and the September 17 FOMC decision. Coming one week after Warsh's inflation warning flipped hike-odds above 50/50, payrolls will be read almost entirely through the "does the labor market give the Fed cover to hike" lens rather than the growth lens. Consensus positioning still leans toward eventual cuts later in the cycle, so a strong beat (e.g., NFP >200k with wage growth firm) would be the asymmetric surprise — it would force a rapid repricing of the 10Y toward the 52-week high (4.74%), pressure the overbought gold/crypto complex hard, and likely trigger the first real VIX spike off 52-week lows. A miss would relieve hike pressure and could extend the equity/gold melt-up further into overbought territory. Given VIX's complacent starting point (14.51, contango intact), the risk/reward into this print favors volatility buyers over directional bets.
