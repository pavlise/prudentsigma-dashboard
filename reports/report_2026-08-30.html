Technical Condition Summary:

- Momentum: RSI readings are unremarkable across equities (SPX 56, NDX 52) — no overbought exhaustion, room to run either direction. The extremes are in crypto: BTC (73) and ETH (72) are both textbook overbought, raising the odds of a sharp mean-reversion pullback if the hawkish-Fed/strong-dollar theme extends. MACD is bearish on SPX/NDX/WTI/Copper even as price holds above key MAs — a classic momentum-lagging-price divergence worth watching for a stall.
- Volatility: VIX at 14.43 (-8.96% 5D) sits in a normal contango term structure (VIX3M 17.48), signalling no near-term hedging panic. ATRs are elevated in commodities (Silver 2.86%, WTI 4.23%) versus compressed equity ATRs (SPX 0.82%, NDX 1.45%) — vol is concentrated in commodities right now, not equities.
- Cross-asset signals: SPX/HYG correlation of +0.79 shows credit and equities moving in lockstep with no divergence — a green flag against imminent equity stress. Gold/DXY correlation flips to -0.45, confirming the gold pullback is dollar-driven rather than a pure risk-off flight (which would show positive correlation). WTI/10Y correlation of +0.62 reinforces that both are being pushed by the same reflation/hawkish-Fed impulse.
- Options sentiment: QQQ put/call volume of 1.30 shows elevated put buying — a bearish/hedging lean specifically in tech, even as SPY's P/C of 1.06 stays neutral. This is a tell that positioning is more defensive under the Nasdaq's surface than the index-level calm suggests.

Sector Rotation: XLK (+5.90% 1M, +2.93% relative), XLV (+5.30%, +2.33% rel) and XLE (+5.26%, +2.29% rel) are leading — a barbell of growth/tech, defensive healthcare, and energy (oil's uptrend). Laggards are the rate-sensitive complex: XLU (-3.65%, -6.62% rel) and XLRE (-1.31%, -4.27% rel), plus XLI (-1.50%, -4.47% rel). This rotation pattern — bond-proxies underperforming while tech leads — is textbook positioning for a market pricing in *higher*, not lower, rates ahead.

HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT: Nonfarm Payrolls (Sep 5) is the pivotal print. With Warsh's Jackson Hole speech having already pushed September hike odds above 50/50 on the inflation side, a strong payrolls print (labor market "stable, growth resilient" per recent Fed commentary) would remove the last argument for a cut and could cement hike pricing — likely spiking the 10Y toward/through its 4.74 52W high and extending DXY strength, which would pressure gold/silver further and could finally bleed into the tech-led equity rally via higher discount rates. A soft print, conversely, would be the first real crack in the hawkish narrative and could trigger a sharp reversal — DXY lower, gold/silver squeeze, and a relief rally in the underperforming rate-sensitive sectors (XLU, XLRE). The asymmetry favors positioning for volatility around this print rather than a single directional bet into it.
