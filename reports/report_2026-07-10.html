SPX/VIX at -0.87 remains textbook, no divergence to flag.

Energy (XLE, -3.79% 1M) and Real Estate (XLRE, -0.78% 1M) lag — energy underperforming even as WTI has rallied 4.39% on 5-day basis is a notable divergence worth watching into next week (equity energy names not yet pricing the crude bounce). This is a broadening rally, not a single-sector melt-up — a healthier signal for the SPX uptrend than pure mega-cap tech leadership.
