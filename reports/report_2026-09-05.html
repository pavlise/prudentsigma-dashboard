HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT — August CPI (Friday, Sept 11): This is the key print standing between markets and the Sept 15-16 FOMC, where hike odds already jumped to ~59-60% after Thursday's blowout payrolls (+162K vs +53K est., unemployment steady at 4.1%, plus +55K in upward June/July revisions). SPX sits just ~1% off its 52-week high with VIX under 15, priced for a mild inflation overshoot to be absorbed painlessly. Asymmetric risk: a hot core print combined with oil-driven cost-push pressure (WTI +59% YTD) would raise hawkish-surprise odds at the FOMC, likely triggering a sharp unwind in duration-sensitive assets (NDX, XLRE, TLT) and pushing the 10Y through its 52W high of 4.80%. A soft print would validate Waller's "hold" narrative and could unlock a fresh equity leg higher — given already-elevated SPY put/call skew (1.44), a soft surprise carries the larger convexity for a risk-on squeeze.
