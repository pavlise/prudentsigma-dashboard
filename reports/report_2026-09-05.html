WTI's RSI of 65 combined with BB%B of 0.95 is the one genuine momentum extreme on the board — oil is stretched and due either a breakout continuation or a sharp mean-reversion. MACD is bearish (rolling over) on SPX, NDX, Gold, Silver, and BTC simultaneously — a broad early-stage momentum deceleration signal even as prices sit near highs, worth watching for confirmation.

SPX/WTI at -0.56 and WTI/10Y at +0.65 together describe the core risk: oil-driven yield pressure is the transmission channel that could break the equity uptrend if it persists. Gold/DXY correlation of -0.58 confirms gold's softness is dollar-driven rather than a risk-off signal — genuine flight-to-safety demand is absent for now.
- Options sentiment: SPY put/call volume of 1.44 (elevated) versus QQQ's neutral 1.07 shows hedging demand concentrated in broad-market/value-tilted SPY exposure rather than tech — consistent with positioning against the oil/rate risk rather than an AI-trade unwind.

XLF and XLV are modest relative outperformers (+1.37% and +3.99% rel-1M) on the earnings beat streak from JPM/GS/UNH. XLI is the laggard (-4.85% rel-1M), and XLK is barely keeping pace with the index despite the mega-cap AI beats — a sign leadership is broadening away from pure tech momentum into energy and financials, a mid-cycle rotation pattern.

HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT: Thursday's CPI print is the single most consequential data point before the Sept 15–16 FOMC decision, now that the blowout August payrolls report (+162K vs +53K est.) has pushed hike odds into the 53–64% range across pricing sources. Market positioning (SPY P/C at 1.44) suggests some hedging is already in place against a hot print. The asymmetric risk: a CPI reading above consensus, layered onto oil's supply-shock-driven surge (WTI +6.7% in 5 days, near 52-week highs) and 10Y yield already brushing its 52-week high of 4.80%, would tip the Fed toward a hike and could trigger a fast repricing across duration-sensitive assets (Gold, growth-tech, TLT) — the correlation data shows 10Y/WTI at +0.65, so oil and inflation surprises are now moving in the same direction, removing the usual offsetting cushion. Conversely, a cool CPI print, especially if oil stabilizes, would validate the current equity uptrend and likely trigger a VIX contango steepening rather than a fear spike.
