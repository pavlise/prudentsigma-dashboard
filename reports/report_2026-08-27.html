With core PCE still tracking ~3.2% y/y and markets pricing low near-term vol (VIX 15.21, contango intact, neutral P/C ratios), positioning going in is light — meaning any directional surprise carries outsized reaction potential. A dovish signal (openness to faster cuts) would likely extend the DXY breakdown (already below all three MAs) and further fuel the overbought gold/crypto complex. A hawkish surprise (emphasis on sticky inflation, caution on rate cuts) risks an outsized unwind in the most stretched positions — Gold (RSI 72), BTC (RSI 81), and ETH (RSI 77) — while offering DXY a relief bounce off its 98.80 20-day low. The Friday NFP annual revision adds a second layer of event risk: a large downward revision (a pattern seen repeatedly in 2025–26) would reinforce a "labor market weaker than headline prints suggest" narrative, complicating Warsh's messaging and amplifying rate-cut repricing.
