HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT — Friday June 6 | Nonfarm Payrolls (May)

May NFP is the single most important release of the week. With the Fed holding
at 3.50–3.75% and explicitly citing labor market resilience as a reason to
pause, this print will either validate or challenge the "no-cut-until-2027"
consensus. April saw unemployment at ~4.3% — any upside surprise (>200K jobs,
unemployment ≤4.2%) would reinforce hawkish hold messaging ahead of the June
16–17 FOMC, likely pressuring TLT and rate-sensitive sectors. A downside miss
(<130K, UE ≥4.5%) could trigger a bond rally and a brief equity dip as
recession fears re-emerge — but given the Iran-driven inflation backdrop, the
"bad news = good news" Fed pivot logic is largely broken for 2026.

Asymmetric risk scenario: A strong payrolls print (+220K+) paired with
average hourly earnings surprising to the upside (+0.4% MoM) would push 10Y
yields back toward the 4.67% 52-week high, pressure gold below SMA200 at
$4,376, and force a short-term correction in both SPX and NDX from overbought
levels. This is the highest-conviction downside scenario for the week.
