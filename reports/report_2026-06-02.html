Dominant Macro Theme Today:
The market enters June in a state of exuberant bifurcation: equities sit at
all-time highs on the back of a 29% YoY S&P 500 EPS growth surge and a
relentless Nvidia-led AI bid (NVDA +2% today on new PC-market chip), yet
the macro backdrop is quietly tightening. April PCE came in at 3.8% YoY —
nearly double the Fed's target — and Middle East tensions around Iran's threat
to close the Strait of Hormuz have driven WTI up +57.7% YTD, inserting a
structural cost-of-energy shock into an already-stubborn inflation regime.
Jobs Week (culminating in Friday's May NFP) will determine whether the Fed's
"higher for longer" stance hardens further, potentially repricing the rate-cut
cycle that equity markets are quietly pricing in for H2 2026.

SECTION 2 — TECHNICAL SNAPSHOT
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Technical Condition Summary:

- Trend structure: SPX and NDX are in clean bull structures — above all three
  moving averages (SMA20/50/200) — but both are pressing the upper Bollinger Band
  (BB%B: 0.92). At these levels, the path of least resistance is a mean-reversion
  pullback to SMA20 (7,431 SPX / 29,276 NDX) before any sustainable leg higher.
  WTI's structure is the mirror image: above SMA200 but below both SMA20 and SMA50,
  confirming the recent -15% monthly pullback is a correction within a larger oil
  bull trend — not a reversal. BTC stands alone in a full bear structure: price
  below all three MAs, with SMA200 at 79,256 now acting as overhead resistance.

- Momentum: SPX RSI 75 and NDX RSI 78 are a yellow flag, not yet a sell signal,
  but breadth is thin — only 2 of 8 S&P sectors outperformed SPX on a 5D basis
  (XLK +4.66% rel, BTC is off-exchange). This is a narrow, momentum-driven rally,
  which historically increases fragility. Copper RSI 66 and price near upper BB
  (0.91) signals genuine industrial demand, not just speculation. BTC RSI 26
  (oversold) and ETH RSI 28 mark crypto as technically due for a bounce, though
  the bear structure argues against new longs without confirmation.

- Volatility: VIX at 16.21 is complacent relative to the macro backdrop, but the
  contango structure (VIX 16.21 → VIX3M 19.43) tells the real story — the market
  is underpricing near-term risk while acknowledging medium-term uncertainty. WTI
  ATR% of 6.66% is the highest in the dashboard, screaming that oil is the most
  volatile and consequential asset this week. Silver's ATR at 3.70% also flags
  high daily range — precision entry/stops matter.

- Cross-asset signals: The SPX/10Y correlation of -0.82 is the most important
  number in the dashboard today. As PCE data Thursday threatens to re-price the
  long end higher (10Y at 4.47%, eyeing 4.67% 52W high), equities face a genuine
  repricing headwind. SPX/HYG at +0.84 is reassuring — credit is not yet
  signaling stress — but if high yield begins to diverge from equities post-NFP,
  that would be an early warning of equity vulnerability. Gold/DXY at -0.67
  confirms gold is currently a dollar-beta trade, not a pure risk-off flight.

- Options sentiment: SPY P/C ratio of 1.07 is neutral, but QQQ P/C at 1.48 is
  a material divergence — large tech is being hedged aggressively even as it leads
  the tape. This is consistent with institutional participants riding the momentum
  while quietly buying downside protection ahead of Jobs Week.

Sector Rotation: The rotation story is stark. XLK (Tech) is the only sector
outperforming SPX on a 5D basis (+4.66% relative), while everything else lags —
Utilities (-5.99% rel), Staples (-2.99%), Energy (-2.03%). The underperformance of
XLE despite WTI's +57.7% YTD gain is particularly notable and suggests the market
is treating the oil spike as a geopolitical event (temporary) rather than a
structural demand story. Real Estate (XLRE -4.30% rel) and Utilities are being
crushed by the elevated 10Y yield at 4.47% — rate-sensitive sectors are being
sold. This is a classic late-cycle tech-momentum rotation: risk appetite is high,
but it is narrowing to a single theme.

HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT — Friday June 6: May Nonfarm Payrolls

This is the week's binary risk event. The market is pricing a resilient but moderating
labor market (~175K–185K consensus expected). Two scenarios:

Scenario A — Beat (>220K, unemployment holds at low levels):
A strong print locks in the Fed's higher-for-longer stance, pushes 10Y yields
toward the 4.67% 52W high, pressures rate-sensitive equity multiples, and
strengthens DXY. Given SPX is already RSI-overbought at 75, a strong jobs report
could be the catalyst for the SMA20 pullback (7,431). Dollar-denominated
commodities (gold, silver) face downside pressure.

Scenario B — Miss (<130K, or unemployment ticks up):
Triggers a dovish Fed pivot narrative re-pricing, bonds rally, 10Y yields drop,
rate-cut expectations return — this is unambiguously bullish for equities, gold,
silver, and crypto. BTC could squeeze hard off its RSI-26 oversold read.

Highest-conviction pre-positioning: The asymmetry argues for holding longs in
assets that benefit from both scenarios (broad quality equities) while avoiding
concentrated bets in rate-sensitive sectors until the NFP print is cleared.

Thursday's PCE is the preview: BofA forecasts Core PCE +0.3% m/m (3.3% y/y) and
headline +0.4% m/m (3.8% y/y). Any upside surprise on headline PCE hardens the
stagflation narrative and could trigger the first meaningful equity pullback since
the current nine-week run-up began.
