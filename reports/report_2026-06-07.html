Dominant Macro Theme Today:
Markets are navigating a "good news is bad news" regime following May's
blowout US jobs print (+172,000, roughly double expectations), which has
pushed the 10Y yield to 4.54% and rekindled fears that new Fed Chairman

exports from Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Kuwait, and the UAE, keeping WTI elevated
at $90.54 (+57.96% YTD) and stoking an inflationary backdrop that makes
Wednesday's May CPI print the highest-impact catalyst of the week.

HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT — Wednesday June 11: US May CPI

May CPI is the week's fulcrum. April CPI was the highest in nearly two
years, jolted by energy (up ~18% YoY) as the Strait of Hormuz closure
rippled through gasoline prices (+28% YoY). With WTI still 57% above
year-ago levels, energy's contribution to May CPI will again be
substantial. Consensus currently expects core CPI to remain sticky around
3.0-3.2% YoY.

Asymmetric scenarios:
- Hot print (Core > 3.3%): 10Y yield tests 4.67% 52W high, DXY
  breaks 100.51, QQQ sells off to SMA50 at 27,446. Gold takes another leg
  lower below its SMA200. Fed rate cut expectations evaporate entirely.
- In-line print (Core 3.0-3.2%): Relief rally in NDX/SPX, short
  covering, VIX retreats toward 18. Dollar pulls back from overbought
  levels. Gold mean-reverts toward 4,450-4,500.
- Cool print (Core < 2.9%): Massive equity rally, BTC bounce, gold
  surges. Very low probability given energy base effects.

Positioning: Given QQQ OI P/C at 1.94 and NDX already -5.1% in 5 days,
a hot or in-line print likely caps any upside. The pain trade is a cool
print triggering a short squeeze.
