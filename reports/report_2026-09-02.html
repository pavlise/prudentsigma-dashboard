MACD is bearish across SPX, NDX, Gold, Silver, and Copper — broad-based momentum deterioration outside of energy and crypto.
- Volatility: WTI's ATR of 3.89% and Silver's 2.93% reflect the stress concentrated in commodities. VIX (16.34) vs VIX3M (18.33) remains in contango — the term structure has not inverted, meaning options markets are not yet pricing sustained crisis, only elevated near-term hedging demand.

Gold/DXY correlation (-0.49) explains why gold is falling even amid war headlines — dollar strength (DXY +0.64% 5D) and rising real yields are outweighing the safe-haven bid, a genuine divergence worth noting for tactical gold positioning.

Healthcare (XLV) is a quieter second leader (+7.26% relative 1M) — a defensive rotation signal. Industrials (XLI) is the worst laggard (-5.98% relative 1M, -3.64% relative 5D), likely pricing higher input costs and freight/shipping-lane risk from the Hormuz conflict. Financials and Staples are roughly in-line with SPX. Net signal: money is rotating into energy and defensives (healthcare) and out of cyclical industrials — a risk-aware, not risk-seeking, rotation.
