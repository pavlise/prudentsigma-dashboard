- Volatility: ATR% is elevated in commodities (WTI 3.93%, Silver 2.97%) versus still-modest equity ATR (SPX 0.82%). - Cross-asset signals: Gold/DXY correlation is solidly negative (-0.62), confirming the current gold weakness is dollar-driven rather than a risk-off signal — genuine flight-to-safety would show gold and DXY rising together. WTI/HYG correlation is strongly negative (-0.79): the oil spike is starting to bite into credit spreads, an early warning that stagflation risk could migrate into corporate credit if crude holds above $100.
- Options sentiment: SPY and QQQ put/call volume ratios (1.53 and 1.55) show pronounced put buying — consistent with hedging into the FOMC binary event rather than outright bearish conviction, since open interest ratios (3.03 / 4.09) show the hedges are being layered on top of existing long positioning.

Sector Rotation: Energy (XLE) is the standout leader, +2.38% relative to SPX over 5 days and +8.81% relative over 1 month, directly tracking the crude spike. Defensive/rate-sensitive sectors are lagging hardest — Healthcare (XLV, -2.85% relative 5D), Industrials (XLI, -0.96% relative 5D, -5.09% relative 1M) and Utilities/Real Estate are underperforming as higher yields compress valuations. This is a classic late-cycle, inflation-scare rotation: capital flowing into energy and out of duration-sensitive and defensive equity proxies.

HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT: The September 16 FOMC decision is the dominant catalyst. With markets now pricing ~85% odds of a 25bp *hike* — a reversal from the cutting cycle narrative that dominated earlier in the year — this is a genuine surprise-risk event. The asymmetry: if the Fed hikes and Powell strikes a hawkish tone (validating the hot CPI/oil-driven inflation concern), expect DXY and yields to extend gains while equities, gold, and long-duration credit sell off further, with NDX most exposed given its already-broken 50-day average. If the Fed instead holds or hikes but signals this is a "one-and-done" response to an oil-driven CPI spike rather than a new tightening cycle, expect a relief rally led by rate-sensitive laggards (XLU, XLRE, XLI) and a sharp VIX unwind from contango levels. Given the geopolitical overlay (Strait of Hormuz risk keeping oil bid regardless of Fed action), the cleaner trade is around volatility and the dollar rather than an outright equity directional bet.
