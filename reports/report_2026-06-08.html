HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT — US CPI May (Wednesday June 10, 08:30 ET):

This is the week's binary catalyst. Consensus expects headline CPI
at +4.2% YoY (vs +4.0% prior), driven by energy (+18% YoY due to
the Iran supply shock) and shelter costs. Core CPI is expected at
+2.8% YoY with a softer +0.2% MoM print as goods disinflation
offsets services stickiness.

Bull scenario (miss / soft print): Core CPI comes in ≤+0.1% MoM.
Markets re-price away from hike expectations; SPX bounces back
toward SMA20 (7,479), DXY fades, gold recovers toward 4,400, NDX
reclaims 29,500. Probability: ~30%.

Bear scenario (beat / hot print): Headline ≥+4.5% YoY or core
≥+0.3% MoM. Warsh hike expectations accelerate; 10Y tests 4.67%
(52W high), SPX breaks toward SMA50 (7,156), DXY breaks above 100.51
(52W high), gold retests 4,250 lows. Probability: ~55%.

Asymmetry: The market is not fully hedged (P/C neutral at 1.00);
a hot print has more shock capacity than a soft one given current
positioning.

Also watch: **Fed Chair Warsh's first FOMC press conference is June
17** — this week's CPI print will directly calibrate how hawkish his
messaging will be one week later.
