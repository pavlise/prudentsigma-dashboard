HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT: The September 16–17 FOMC decision is the week's pivotal risk. Market pricing has been genuinely two-sided into this meeting: the oil-driven inflation impulse (WTI +74.5% YTD, feeding into headline CPI) is pushing back against the case for a cut just as the 10Y sits at a 52-week high (4.97%), while softening growth data (SPX/NDX below SMA20, elevated equity put/call ratios) argues the Fed still has room to ease. A cut delivered alongside dovish forward guidance would be read as validating a soft landing and could compress the 10Y sharply, benefiting equities and gold; a hold, or a cut paired with hawkish "one-and-done" language citing oil-driven inflation risk, would likely extend the yield rise and pressure both equities and rate-sensitive sectors (Utilities, REITs) further. Given the elevated SPY/QQQ put/call skew already in place, positioning suggests the market is hedged for the hawkish-surprise scenario — meaning a dovish outcome carries the larger asymmetric squeeze potential.
