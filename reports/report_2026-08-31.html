Dominant Macro Theme Today:
Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole speech (delivered Aug 28, still reverberating) has repriced September rate-hike odds sharply higher — fed funds futures now show ~60% probability of a hike at the mid-September FOMC, up from ~56% a session earlier ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/31/jackson-hole-fed-chair-kevin-warsh-hawkish-rate-hikes-analysts.html), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/warsh-s-inflation-warning-sets-up-september-showdown-for-the-fed)). Simultaneously, Middle East tensions around the Strait of Hormuz continue to underpin crude, complicating the inflation fight the Fed is trying to win. Equities are digesting a strong August (S&P +3%, Nasdaq +4% MTD) against this newly hawkish backdrop, with Friday's session seeing indices slip as chip names gave back gains.

Market Regime Today: Mixed / Cautious Consolidation
Conviction Level: Medium
Reason: Equities remain in an intact bull structure (SPX/NDX above 50/200 SMA) but are consolidating just under 20-day averages after a strong August. The Warsh-driven repricing of rate-hike odds is lifting yields and the dollar while pressuring gold — a classic hawkish-Fed rotation — yet VIX at 15 and SPX/VIX correlation near -0.67 (normal) show no fear has entered equity positioning yet. Oil's geopolitical bid adds a second inflationary variable the Fed must weigh, creating two-way risk into the September FOMC.

The outlier is crypto: BTC RSI 72 and SOL RSI 72, both flagged overbought, while MACD stays bullish — momentum extended but not yet turning. MACD is bearish on SPX/NDX/Gold/WTI/Copper despite price near highs, a mild negative divergence worth monitoring into next week's data.
- Volatility: VIX at 15.23 with the term structure in contango (VIX3M 17.48) signals no near-term stress being priced — normal risk regime. ATR% is elevated in commodities (WTI 4.04%, Silver 2.85%) versus compressed equity ATR (SPX 0.82%), consistent with the volatility currently concentrated in the inflation-sensitive complex rather than equities.
- Cross-asset signals: SPX/HYG correlation is strongly positive (+0.78) with HYG holding firm — credit is not flashing stress ahead of equities. Gold/DXY correlation is notably negative-lean structurally (-0.53), confirming gold's pullback is dollar-driven (Warsh hawkishness) rather than a pure risk-off unwind. WTI/HYG at -0.73 is the most extreme cross-asset reading — rising energy costs squeezing credit sentiment bears watching if oil extends its Middle East-driven rally.

Sector Rotation: Over the past month, XLK (Tech, +5.90%, +2.93% relative) and XLE (Energy, +5.26%, +2.29% relative) are the clear leaders — a barbell of AI-driven growth and energy/inflation beneficiaries that fits the current macro narrative. XLU (Utilities, -3.65%) and XLI (Industrials, -1.50%) lag most, alongside XLRE — classic underperformance of rate-sensitive defensives as yields climb on Warsh's hawkish signal. This rotation supports a "higher-for-longer" regime rather than a growth-scare regime.

HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT: Friday's Nonfarm Payrolls (Sep 4) is the single most consequential print before the mid-September FOMC. Consensus/prediction-market expectations point to ~46K jobs added, up from a soft 22K in August ([Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/35164863/how-many-jobs-will-the-u-s-economy-add-in-september)) — a low bar that a moderate beat would clear easily. Given Warsh has already primed markets for a hike, a strong payrolls + firm wages print would push hike odds well above 60% and likely extend the DXY/10Y rally while pressuring gold, silver, and rate-sensitive equity sectors (XLU, XLRE, XLI) further. Conversely, a miss below 22K would reopen "soft landing/pause" pricing, likely triggering a sharp reversal — 10Y yield back toward SMA50, gold/silver relief rally, and a rotation back into growth/tech. Asymmetric risk: given how consensus is already leaning hawkish post-Jackson Hole, a soft print carries the larger surprise/repricing potential.
