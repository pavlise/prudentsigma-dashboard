Healthcare (+3.5%/+4.0% relative) and Financials (+0.9%/+1.4% relative) are showing quiet, steady outperformance, consistent with a market rotating toward defensives/value rather than chasing high-multiple growth into the CPI print.

HIGHEST IMPACT EVENT: Friday's August CPI report is the pivotal print of the week. With the FOMC already in its quiet period ahead of the September 16 decision, and futures markets having pared cut expectations to roughly one 2026 cut on the back of the oil-driven inflation scare, CPI is the last hard data point the Fed sees before deciding. A soft core CPI print (in line with or below consensus) would revive rate-cut odds and likely trigger a relief rally in growth/tech (NDX) and pressure the 10Y yield lower from its 4.78% ledge near 52-week highs. A hot print — plausible given WTI's 14.6% one-month surge feeding through to energy-sensitive CPI components — would be the asymmetric risk: it could push the 10Y through 4.80%, pressure equity multiples (SPX sits right at its SMA20 support of 7,708.70), and validate the SPY market's already-elevated 1.47 put/call positioning. Given oil's outsized recent move, skew favors a hotter-than-expected headline number, making downside protection into Friday's print the more prudent stance.
