Technical Condition Summary:
- Trend structure: SPX holds clean bull structure — above SMA20/SMA50/SMA200, +8.2% over SMA200. NDX is more fragile: above SMA20 and SMA200 but -0.1% below its SMA50, an early sign mega-cap tech leadership is cooling even as the broader index holds. Gold and WTI show textbook bull structure (price > SMA20 > SMA50 > SMA200); Silver is the outlier — bullish short-term but still -2.1% below SMA200, so the metals complex isn't uniformly confirming a durable regime shift.
- Momentum: RSI dispersion is wide. DXY (30) is oversold — a dollar bounce is the highest-probability mean-reversion trade on the board. Gold (76), BTC (80), and ETH (78) are all overbought, signaling crowded longs in the "debasement trade." SPX (54) and NDX (50) sit neutral with Bearish MACD crossovers on both — momentum has stalled, not reversed.
- Volatility: VIX 15.13 with VIX3M 18.50 keeps the term structure in healthy contango — options aren't pricing near-term panic despite the Iran headlines. ATR% confirms the split: equities stay low-vol (SPX 0.90%, NDX 1.58%) while commodities/crypto run hot (WTI 4.14%, BTC 2.68%, Silver 2.66%) — realized risk is concentrated exactly where the geopolitical and rate narratives collide.
- Cross-asset signals: SPX/HYG correlation of +0.84 is intact — a genuine equity-negative shock would show up in credit first, and it hasn't. Gold/DXY correlation is only -0.45, weaker than the classic inverse relationship, implying gold's rally is driven more by pure risk-off/geopolitical demand than dollar weakness — relevant for how durable the move is once DXY bounces from oversold.
- Options sentiment: SPY (1.16) and QQQ (1.06) put/call volume are neutral — no extreme hedging or complacency. OI ratios (SPY 1.05, QQQ 1.15) show slightly heavier structural put positioning in tech, consistent with the NDX/SMA50 wobble above.

Sector Rotation: Healthcare (XLV, +5.44% rel. 5D) and Energy (XLE, +2.61% rel. 5D) lead, with Staples (+2.46% rel.) confirming a defensive bid. Utilities (-2.28% rel., -11.14% rel. 1M) and Industrials (-2.35% rel.) are sold hardest, alongside Tech (-2.77% rel. 5D) losing relative strength for the first time in weeks. Net signal: flows rotating out of rate-sensitive/high-beta growth into Energy/Healthcare — a hedge against the Iran oil shock and rate uncertainty into Jackson Hole, not yet full risk-off capitulation.
